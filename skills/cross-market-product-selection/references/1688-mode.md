## Source order

Use the strongest available 1688-specific source for each field:

1. configured Sorftime 1688 data or workflow;
2. current 1688 item detail and supplier pages through an authorized browser session;
3. user-provided item URLs, exports, screenshots, or files;
4. another trustworthy 1688-specific source tied to the exact item ID.

Do not use SerpApi as a 1688 product-detail source. General web snippets may help discover a URL but cannot verify a hard gate or supplier fact.

## Retrieval coverage

Create a coverage matrix that includes, when available:

- core and synonym keyword searches;
- material, form, process, use-case, and customization terms;
- image search from reference products;
- similar-item discovery;
- same-store expansion;
- category and regional variants;
- explicit negative terms for accessories, components, or unwanted materials.

Record each path and depth as attempted, unavailable, or skipped with reason. A limited result set from one keyword is not “all relevant products.” Expand high-yield terms and paths until the agreed scope, time, or page budget is reached.

Deduplicate by 1688 item ID or canonical item URL. Similar titles from different suppliers remain separate offers.

## Candidate and supplier identity

At minimum, retain:

- 1688 item ID and canonical item URL;
- raw title and product image;
- selected SKU or variation context;
- supplier profile URL and stable supplier ID when available;
- actual store or company name only when a reliable source returns it;
- discovery query and path;
- retrieval timestamp.

Never turn an image-owner ID, member ID, numeric code, domain fragment, or missing interface value into a store name. If the store name is not returned, leave it blank and keep the stable ID in its own field.

## Detail verification

Apply hard gates before scoring:

1. confirm the listing is the target product body rather than an accessory, component, package, or service;
2. open or retrieve the exact item detail when required;
3. verify required material, dimensions, process, compatibility, and customization from acceptable evidence;
4. verify SKU-level price, MOQ, color, size, and material context;
5. inspect actual product images when form or finish matters;
6. confirm supplier facts from the supplier profile or another authoritative source.

If a title says one material but the detail page says another, use the detail evidence and record the conflict. A verified mismatch rejects the item; unresolved evidence makes it pending.

Price ranges must retain their quantity or SKU conditions. Do not present the lowest tier as a generally available unit price without its threshold.

## Common fields

Collect only fields needed for the decision. Typical fields include:

- item ID, title, URL, main image, category;
- SKU or variation, material, color, dimensions, process;
- price range, currency, quantity tier, MOQ, sample terms;
- customization, logo, packaging, lead time, and shipping evidence;
- supplier name, supplier ID, profile URL, years or badges when reliably available;
- transaction, repeat-purchase, response, or other supplier metrics from an approved source;
- raw values, source URLs, timestamps, confidence, and conflicts.

Leave absent weight, dimensions, MOQ, store name, or supplier metrics blank. Do not estimate a required hard-gate field unless the user explicitly allows an estimate, and even then keep it separate from verified values.

## Status and ranking

Only strict items enter the ranked shortlist. Pending rows remain candidates for targeted verification. Rejected rows retain the exact failed gate and evidence.

If discovery was incomplete because image search, similar items, same-store expansion, detail pages, or supplier profiles were unavailable, disclose the missing paths and avoid claiming exhaustive coverage.
